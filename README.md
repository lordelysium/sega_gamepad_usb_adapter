Pinout for me:

1 pad (controller):

pin 3 - DB9 Pin 7 (Select)

pin 9 - DB9 Pin 1 (Up)

pin 8 - DB9 Pin 2 (Down)

pin 7 - DB9 Pin 3 (Left)

pin 6 - DB9 Pin 4 (Right)

pin 4 - DB9 Pin 6 (Button A/B)

pin 5 - DB9 Pin 9 (Start/C)

2 pad (controller_2):

pin A2 - DB9 Pin 7 (Select)

pin 10 - DB9 Pin 1 (Up)

pin 16 - DB9 Pin 2 (Down)

pin 14 - DB9 Pin 3 (Left)

pin 15 - DB9 Pin 4 (Right)

pin A1 - DB9 Pin 6 (Button A/B)

pin A0 - DB9 Pin 9 (Start/C)


Power (both):

DB9 Pin 5 - VCC (+5V)

DB9 Pin 8 - GND
